Cogs & Crates: Factory Rush - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy covers the game Cogs & Crates: Factory Rush on Android, iPhone, iPad and Amazon devices. It is published by NewAge Interactive ("we", "us").

Information we do not collect

The game has no sign-in and no player accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or payment details. The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.

Your saved game

Your progress is stored only on your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes the save. A device backup made by Google or Apple falls under that provider's own policy.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in return for a bonus.

  • Android and Amazon devices: ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google may collect your advertising identifier, IP address, device type and operating system, and information about how you interact with ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • iPhone and iPad: ads are served through Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), which collects similar information under Unity's Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time under Menu, Privacy choices. Elsewhere, ads may be personalised unless you turn that off there. On iPhone and iPad an App Tracking Transparency prompt appears; declining it does not remove the ad rewards, only personalisation. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings. Buying "No ads" removes the ad offers.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play, the Apple App Store or the Amazon Appstore. We never see or store your payment details. The store only tells the game which items you own.

Children

The game is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. Ads shown in the game are limited to general-audience content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete. For data held by an advertising provider (Google or Unity), use that provider's privacy controls or your device's advertising settings. For any other question, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
