Privacy Policy

Cogs & Crates: Factory Rush by NewAge Interactive

Last updated: 25 October 2026 (ads now provided by Unity Ads)

This policy explains what information is used when you play Cogs & Crates: Factory Rush (“the game”) on Android, iPhone, iPad or Amazon devices. In short: we do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not collect personal information ourselves. The game shows optional rewarded ads through Unity Ads and sells optional in-app purchases through your app store; those services handle their own data as described below.

Information we do not collect

  • The game has no sign-in and no player accounts.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or payment details.
  • The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Your saved game

Your progress (your factories, cash, crew and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this save. If your device backs up app data (for example through your Google or Apple backup), that backup is handled by your device’s provider under their privacy policy.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch an ad in return for an in-game reward. The ads are provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To show and measure ads, Unity may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads. Unity uses this under its own policies:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads are not personalised unless you choose to allow it. Everywhere else they may be personalised unless you choose not to. You can change this at any time under Menu, Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for permission to track through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose not to allow it, you still get every ad reward; ads are just less personalised.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings.
  • Buying “No ads” in the game’s store removes the ad offers.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Google Play, the Apple App Store or the Amazon Appstore. We never see or store your payment details. Your app store tells the game only which items you own, so it can unlock them, under that store’s own privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information Unity holds for advertising, see Unity’s privacy policy above or your device’s ad settings. If you have any question or request, contact us below and we will answer.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it uses information, we will update this page and its date above.
